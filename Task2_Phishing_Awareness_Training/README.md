# Task 2: Phishing Awareness Training
CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship | Mahmoud Mohamed Sayed

A complete phishing awareness training module:

- `Phishing_Awareness_Training.pptx` - 12-slide presentation covering what
  phishing is, the social-engineering psychology behind it, how to recognize
  phishing emails and fake websites, two annotated real-world examples (a fake
  bank alert email and a fake delivery SMS), other channels (smishing, vishing,
  QR codes), protection best practices, and what to do after a mistake. Ends
  with a quiz and an answer key.
- `phishing_quiz.html` - standalone interactive quiz (8 questions, instant
  feedback and scoring). Open in any browser; no server or internet needed.

## Slide outline
1. Title
2. What is phishing?
3. Why phishing works: social engineering
4. Anatomy of a phishing email (red flags)
5. How to spot a fake website
6. Real-world example: fake bank alert
7. Real-world example: fake delivery SMS (smishing)
8. Phishing beyond email
9. Best practices
10. Incident response: if you clicked
11. Quiz
12. Quiz answers and key takeaways
